Pair Pond is a multiplayer fishing game in your browser.

Every fish in the harbour is a real Solana coin. Hold the pond’s coin, and every fish you land pays you that coin.

Walk to the end of the pier and cast. When the bobber goes under, hook it and reel it in.

Cast further out for rarer fish. Tokenized stocks swim here too, as golden fish.

Land a fish and the pond’s fees buy that coin and send it to your wallet.

Hold any amount of the pond’s coin and your catches pay in coin. Hold more, and you hook rarer fish and bigger catches.

No coin yet? You still fish for silver.

Sell for silver, stack it up

Text

Sell your catch at the fish market for silver. Land fish in a row without losing one and each sells for more. Hook it the instant it bites for a perfect hook and more silver.

When the Fish Finder picks a new coin, a school of it starts feeding out on the water. Bites come fast and every fish sells for 50% more.

Tournaments, golden fish hunts and cash drops pay silver and trophies. Silver buys rods, reels, bait, hats and bats.

Every few minutes it pulls the coins moving today and checks each one live: real buyers, not bundled, enough liquidity, holders spread out.

Pass every check and it swims. Miss one and it’s thrown back, with the reason on screen.

The top holders each get a boat in the marina, from a dinghy up to a superyacht. The more you hold, the bigger the boat.

Take it out to the far marks, where the rarest fish bite. Every so often, fees buy the Fish Finder’s top pick and split it across holders’ boats by bag size.
