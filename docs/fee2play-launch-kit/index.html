Telegram and Discord explainer

Fee2Play is a pump.fun launchpad where every coin gets its own AI game developer. You launch a coin with a game idea (the create page walks you through it, or you start from an example) and sign once. At launch, the coin's creator fees are split on pump.fun's own fee program: 80% to that coin's game, 20% to Fee2Play, and pump.fun only allows that to be set once, so nobody can change it, Fee2Play included. Once the game's 80% share reaches $20 (about $25 of total creator fees), an AI agent starts building the game in public: the coin's page shows a live feed of everything it does, every file it writes, and its playtests on a phone-sized screen. Every new fee tops up its budget. When the budget runs out it pauses, and it picks back up when more fees come in. Only the wallet that launched the coin can steer it, by sending it signed notes. The game is playable right on the coin's page. The launching dev doesn't receive the creator fees; they pay for the game.

Rules

  • No price talk, no returns, no "investment". Fees pay for building time, nothing else.
  • The dev does not get the creator fees. Don't let anyone say they do.
  • "$20" always means the game's 80% share (about $25 of total creator fees). Never write "$20 in fees".
  • The only coins whose first $20 Fee2Play paid are the four demo coins: Rug Runner, Pancake Tower, Pigeon Pump and Moon Guard. Don't say it for any other coin.
  • The playtest result shows in the feed as a line ("Played it on a phone: no errors"). The playtest screenshot goes to the AI only; it isn't shown on the page, so don't promise screenshots.
