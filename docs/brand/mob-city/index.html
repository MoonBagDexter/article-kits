Using it

A few rules

  • Spell it MOB CITY, two words, one line. Never stacked or arched.
  • Never stretch, recolour or redraw the wordmark. Scale it whole.
  • On dark backgrounds use the gold. On light or gold backgrounds use a one-colour version.
  • Text sitting on a gold fill is always dark, never white.
  • Headings in Oswald, body text in Barlow (both free on Google Fonts).
